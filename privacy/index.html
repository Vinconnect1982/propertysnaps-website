How we use your information

Privacy & enquiries.

Property Snaps collects only the information needed to answer your enquiry and supply requested resources.

Contact us ↗

Your information

When you submit an enquiry or request guides, we may collect your name, work email, telephone number, company, suburb, service requirements and the message you provide. We use this information to answer your request, prepare quotes, arrange service and deliver requested guides.

Contact preferences

Guide downloads include an optional checkbox for future contact. We will not treat a guide download as permission to send unrelated marketing without that choice.

Where information goes

Forms on this website are processed using Netlify Forms. Information may be handled by Netlify or relevant service providers to operate the site. Please avoid sending sensitive documents or personal records through the standard enquiry form.

Your questions

To ask about personal information submitted through the website, request correction or deletion, contact Property Snaps using the contact details on our contact page.

Website analytics

This launch version does not install third-party advertising trackers. External font resources may be requested to display the site styling. If tracking systems are added later this page should be updated.
