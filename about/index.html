Our story

From a camera on a 20-metre pole to the modern property campaign.

Founded by Vince De Stefano in 2006, Property Snaps began by exploring ways to bring new perspectives to property marketing. One of its early ideas was a custom elevated camera pole, built at a time when low-level aerial property photography was far less common.

Over the years the business grew beyond still photography into floor plans, drone imagery, video and creative marketing. Today the focus is purposefully local: quality media, practical communication and dependable support for agents and builders in Melbourne’s south-east.

Our aim remains simple: make good properties, interesting spaces and carefully completed work look as compelling as they deserve.

How we work

A little more thought in every frame.

We consider the listing purpose, the property’s strongest features and the practical information that buyers, clients and guests need. The result is media designed to work, not simply decorate a page.
