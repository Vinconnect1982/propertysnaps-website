Frequently asked questions

Practical answers before the shoot.

Clear information for agents, builders and property managers planning photography, drone, measured plans and video.

Discuss your project ↗
What areas do you service?

We focus on Casey and Cardinia, including Cranbourne, Berwick, Narre Warren, Pakenham, Officer, Clyde and neighbouring suburbs. Ask about other locations.

Can I book photography and a floor plan together?

Yes. We can scope photography, aerial images, floor plans and video as a coordinated property-media brief.

Do you offer twilight photography?

Yes. True twilight sessions suit properties with exterior lighting, quality landscaping and strong street appeal. Availability depends on weather, access and timing.

Can you provide measured floor plans or CAD?

Measured marketing floor plans, 2D drawings, 3D perspectives and CAD-style support may be available by agreed scope. Marketing plans are not land surveys or statutory certification.

Do you photograph finished builds or pools?

Yes. Property Snaps creates photography and media for home builders, designers, specialist trades, pool builders and landscape companies.

Can you fly a drone at any property?

Drone services depend on weather, permission, nearby airspace and applicable regulations. We check suitability as part of the brief.

Do you publish service pricing?

No. We quote by property type, access requirements, location and deliverables so you receive an accurate current price.

How do I request a quote?

Use the enquiry form and include the property suburb, timeline and services required. We will respond to discuss the scope.
