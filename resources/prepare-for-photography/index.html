Preparing your home for photography.

A few practical steps before the shoot help produce stronger, more consistent listing media.

Before the appointment

The small details that matter.

Clean countertops and mirrors, open blinds, turn on appropriate lights, tidy garden edges, move bins and cars from hero angles, and secure pets. The photographer can then focus on capturing the property.

If your campaign includes twilight photography, check that external and garden lighting is functioning before the appointment.

Quick preparation checklist

  • Remove personal paperwork and clutter
  • Clear kitchen benches and bathroom surfaces
  • Make beds and straighten soft furnishings
  • Tidy lawns, paving and outdoor entertaining areas
  • Arrange access to all rooms
  • Advise us of special features or restricted access
